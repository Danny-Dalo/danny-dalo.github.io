I messaged multiple super agents at 3 AM UAE time. The earliest response came roughly three hours later.

That made me wonder: what happens to a new lead when the agent is asleep, busy, or simply too slow to respond?

So I built something that makes sure a new lead doesn't have to wait that long.

How it works

1

2

3
Everything is saved to your CRM. The lead receives an instant response and stays warm until you are ready to take over.
